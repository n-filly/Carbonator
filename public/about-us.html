About Us & Our Project

We are Phuc and Nawal, two students who share a love for math and problem-solving. For our software development summer final project, we wanted to create something that combines our interest in numbers with a real-world purpose. A carbon calculator felt like the perfect fit because it allowed us to use math and programming to turn everyday information into meaningful data.

Our application allows users to enter values for electricity, vehicles, fuel, and shipping to estimate their carbon emissions. We chose to use the CarbonSutra API because it gave us a way to calculate these emissions within our application while allowing us to focus on building a simple and useful experience for users. Our goal was to make carbon emissions easier to understand and show how everyday practices can contribute to a person's environmental impact.
